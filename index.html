Your kit

  • Insta360 Ace Pro 2 — flip screen, high frame rate, voice control
  • Selfie stick — keep it short, it shows in shot
  • AirPods as mic — test first; camera mics as backup
  • 2–3 spare batteries or a USB-C car charger
  • A cleared, high-capacity microSD card

Before you go

Book a room near Bakewell for Sunday night — Bank Holiday, fills fast
Charge every battery and your AirPods
Clear the microSD card
Test the AirPods as a mic with the camera
Set phone alarms for Saturday's "leave by" times

What to Talk About

Learn two or three points per stop, then say them in your own words — don't read a script.

On cameraYou're not presenting — you're telling a mate about your trip. Say what you're about to do, do it, then react. Keep each clip 20–40 seconds. Fluffing your words is fine; pause and go again.
Cold open
Film in the car or at Lavenham
  • Who you are — first-ever episode of WockingTocking
  • What this is: Essex to Leeds, three days, the scenic way
  • The promise: old towns, a city, the Peak District
Lavenham
Angle: a perfect medieval town
  • Point at how wonky it all is — the timber was used unseasoned, so it warped as it dried
  • 600 years ago this was one of England's richest towns, all on wool
  • De Vere House was Godric's Hollow in Harry Potter
Stamford
Angle: timber to stone
  • All one honey-coloured limestone — that's why it's so harmonious
  • England's first-ever Conservation Area, 1967
  • It looks like a film set because it is one — Pride & Prejudice (2005)
Lincoln
Angle: the big one — and a hill
  • Steep Hill — make a joke of the climb
  • The cathedral was the tallest building in the world for over 200 years, until its spire fell in 1548
  • The castle holds an original 1215 Magna Carta
York
Angle: a walled city, just you
  • Lean into the solo tone — calmer, more reflective
  • The Shambles is said to have inspired Diagon Alley
  • The most complete city walls in England — walk a stretch on camera
  • A short honest reflection somewhere quiet: how the trip's going
Bakewell
Angle: arrived at the Peak
  • The Bakewell pudding and the tart are different things — the pudding is the original
  • Buy one, try it on camera, react honestly
Castleton & Winnats Pass
Angle: old towns to ancient land
  • This limestone formed in a tropical sea — it was all underwater once
  • Mam Tor, the "Shivering Mountain" — its old road was abandoned in 1979 as the hill kept slipping
  • Castleton is the only place on earth you find Blue John stone
Closing sign-off
A Peak viewpoint or Bakewell
  • One-sentence recap and your single highlight
  • What it meant — first episode done, you did it
  • The ask: like, subscribe — and "where should episode 2 go?"

Golden rules

  • Film more than you think you need
  • Say the place name on camera at every stop
  • Get the establishing shot the moment you arrive
  • Talk like a person, not a tour guide
  • Never film while driving
